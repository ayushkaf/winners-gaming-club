Symbols & payouts

Both models play 30 fixed lines, left to right, at 30 credits per spin (1 credit per line). Pays are per line bet. The Bull is wild in Golden Charge and substitutes for every paying symbol; the Gate scatter and Gold Coin are never substituted. In Thunder Herd the Money Bull carries a credit value (or a MINI / MINOR / MEGA label) and only pays through the Hold & Respin feature.

Model comparison

Two deliberately different volatility designs sharing one symbol set and line game. Golden Charge spreads its return across line pays, collect events and free games. Thunder Herd concentrates almost two-thirds of its return into a feature that hits about once every 121 spins — the classic hold-&-respin profile: long quiet stretches, then a large, highly variable payout.

"Exact / solved RTP" is the mathematically derived return of the tuned model (closed-form where possible, plus a 16-million-feature structure estimate for Thunder Herd's feature). "Measured" is an independent Monte Carlo run of the finished engines. See the validation reports in the repository for full detail.

How the features work

Golden Charge — the double collect

Whenever two or more Bulls are visible after a spin, a Charge Hit fires: each Bull separately collects the sum of every visible Gold Coin. Two Bulls means the full coin sum is paid twice; three Bulls, three times. After collecting, every Bull flips into a silver coin and reveals its own extra prize — a credit amount or a jackpot. Thunder Herd — collectors, diamonds, and the Stampede phase

Six or more Money Bulls lock in place and start 3 respins on a 15-position grid. Every respin, each empty position can land a new prize — and any new prize resets the counter to 3. Two special landings change the shape of the feature: a Collector Bull arrives carrying the sum of everything already revealed (then sticks like a normal prize), and a Diamond Bull is a normal prize that also adds one extra grid position, played in a second Stampede phase after the main grid settles. Fill all 15 main positions — in either phase — or land a prize in every column during the Stampede phase, and the GRAND (1,000× bet) is paid on top.
